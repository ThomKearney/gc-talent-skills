* **Global Benchmark and Relativity Analysis:** Monica and Thomas explored the use of global benchmark comparisons and relativity analysis, explaining how to summarize overall job equivalency and compare positions internally and externally for classification consistency.
  + **Global Benchmark Comparison:** Monica explained that, in addition to factor-by-factor comparisons, a global benchmark comparison summarizes the overall equivalency of the job to a benchmark, typically in a concise paragraph, which is also performed by their AI system.
